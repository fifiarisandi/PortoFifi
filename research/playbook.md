# AI-Powered SEO Content Production — Playbook / SOP

## Recommendations

- Rewrite heavily and infuse personal experience, anecdotes, case studies, and accuracy review; more original rewriting = better performance.
(source: Semrush, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a4jmsmdtvfo, 21.03.2024)

- Four structuring principles: BLUF (bottom-line-up-front), atomic content (each H2 section stands alone), entity-rich writing (specific brands/products/names), keep it simple, declarative sentences (one idea per sentence).
(source: Ahrefs, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OccF4g0UKI, 13.05.2026)

- Content length doesn't matter for AEO.
(source: Ahrefs, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OccF4g0UKI, 13.05.2026)

- Freshness matters a lot. You need to make meaningful updates to the actual content.
(source: Ahrefs, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OccF4g0UKI, 13.05.2026)

- LLMs have a tendency to flatten originality. The way around this is to label your ideas with your brand name. For example, instead of calling something a content scoring matrix, call it the Ahrefs content scoring matrix. Define it explicitly and then distribute it widely across your blog, social media channels, podcasts, Reddit, etc. The more places it shows up with your name attached, the harder it is for AI to flatten it into generic knowledge.
(source: Ahrefs, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OccF4g0UKI, 13.05.2026)

- Refresh sleeper pages. These are pages on your site that used to rank well, but have slowly declined over time. Because freshness is such a strong signal for AI citations, updating these pages can be one of the fastest ways to gain AI visibility.
(source: Ahrefs, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OccF4g0UKI, 13.05.2026)

- Don't prompt ChatGPT to "write an SEO optimized blog post" and publish — this violates E-E-A-T and Google's AI spam guidelines.
(source: WPBeginner, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cxa0dlnElc8, 17.03.2025)

- Draft prompt specifies: conversational empathetic tone, 8th-grade reading level, 4–5 sentences max per paragraph, no excessive bullet points, goal is helpful not ranking.
(source: WPBeginner, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cxa0dlnElc8, 17.03.2025)

- Manually edit and fact-check each AI-generated post, cutting 20–60% of AI fluff; add custom media and specialist language.
(source: HubSpot Marketing, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kuhhJ4PA8gk, 01.05.2024)

- Obvious AI writing increases pogo-sticking (bounce back to SERP), hurting rankings and conversions.
(source: Edward Sturm, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8CoElpldtk, 30.03.2026)

- AI miscategorization reveals audience misconceptions. If AI compares you to wrong competitors, part of your market likely believes the same. Three-step response: (1) find what AI gets right, (2) identify misconceptions/wrong category placement, (3) turn gaps into priorities for website copy, content, PR, sales enablement, and category education; re-run in 1–2 months.
(source: Amanda Natividad, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/amandanat_aeo-grader-2026-share-7470862206637891584-oOQy/, 12.06.2026)

- Use AI for content strategy tasks that go beyond writing: auditing CTAs, topic research, article editing, finding distribution partners, and performance analysis.
(source: Andy Crestodina, https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/ai-content-strategy/, 08.06.2026)

- The three types of content that consistently outperform all others: original research (new data), strong opinion (thought leadership), and topics not yet covered. The lowest performers are generic how-to articles with no unique data or perspective.
(source: Andy Crestodina, https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/ai-content-strategy/, 08.06.2026)

- Align topics with channels: search users are busy (want answers), social users are bored (want to be surprised) — different content types perform better in different channels.
(source: Andy Crestodina, https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/ai-content-strategy/, 08.06.2026)
